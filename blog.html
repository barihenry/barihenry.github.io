(unknown date) Throughout my life I've always been "kinda" productive. Like I'd put in enough effort to get by in my classes but I never felt that I was living up to my potential. Sometimes I'd declaratively cognize that I was working either "as hard as I can" or measured my output with respect to others (either those around me or some other quasi-canonical measure) and conclude that I was doing just fine. But at the back of my head I had the slight inkling that I was sorta lying to myself. That there was an untapped potential within myself that I hadn't yet made real. Now, I want to choose my words carefully here. Idk if it'd be accurate to say that I was/am "afraid" of my potential. Or "hesitant". Or "disappointed". These words convey particular things that I'm not sure describe my situation-- arbitrating that is perhaps the job of a psychologist. What I can say is that I had these inklings and exhibited the behaviors of doing just enough to get by. Plenty of procrastination and cramming was a part of that. This has been a running theme throughout my life and I'd venture to say that it's the main problem in my life up to and including this point. Other aspects of my life are pretty vicissitudinal in comparison, to be honest, even if you include my (to be honest, pretty nominal) achievements so far (euphonium and quizbowl and ucla). I want this to change. It's not super clear to me how I should go about it.
